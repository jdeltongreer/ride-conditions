How the ride score works

Each hour starts at 100 and loses points as follows (all thresholds are in the CFG block at the top of the script):

    Light comes from the computed solar elevation at mid-hour: day (sun ≥ 6°), low sun (0–6°, golden hour and glare risk for drivers), twilight (civil, 0 to −6°), dark. Weather and wind come from Open-Meteo (best-match models). AQI and PM come from Open-Meteo Air Quality (CAMS) and may be blank for later days.
